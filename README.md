# Quacner

A simple QR and barcode app for Android.

Quacner lets you make QR codes and barcodes, scan them with your camera, and
save them to your phone. It works for links, plain text, and WiFi details.

## What you can do

- Make a QR code for a link or any text
- Make a WiFi QR code so guests can join your network by scanning it
- Make a barcode
- Scan a code with your camera
- Scan a code from a photo or screenshot
- Change the colours of your code
- Save your codes, and look back at what you've made before

## How to install

1. Copy the Quacner app file to your phone.
2. Tap it. If your Camera asks, allow installing apps from this source.
3. Tap **Install**.
4. Open **Quacner** from your apps.

## How to use

- Open the app and type your link or text — a QR code appears straight away.
- Tap the big round button at the bottom to scan a code with your camera.
- Use the save button to keep a code, or the copy button to copy its text.

## Good to know

- The app needs an internet connection the first time you use it.
- Your codes are made on your phone — nothing is sent anywhere.
- This is a personal app, so it isn't on the Play Store.
